# Seed Prompts for Subset C (Boundary Relevance)

## In-Domain Direct Factual
Generate direct factual questions clearly within the ZI domain.

Example:
{"query": "Apa definisi Zona Integritas menurut PermenPANRB?", "label": "in_domain", "subtype": "direct_factual"}

## In-Domain Procedural
Generate procedural questions within the ZI domain.

Example:
{"query": "Bagaimana prosedur pengajuan laporan kinerja dalam ZI?", "label": "in_domain", "subtype": "procedural"}

## In-Domain Complex
Generate complex multi-hop questions within the ZI domain.

Example:
{"query": "Bagaimana korelasi antara area perubahan dan LKE dalam evaluasi ZI?", "label": "in_domain", "subtype": "complex"}

## Out-of-Domain Off-Topic
Generate questions completely unrelated to ZI or bureaucratic reform.

Example:
{"query": "Berapa harga Bitcoin hari ini?", "label": "out_of_domain", "subtype": "off_topic"}

## Out-of-Domain Adjacent
Generate questions that are adjacent to ZI but not covered by the KB (e.g.,
general governance, other countries' anti-corruption programs).

Example:
{"query": "Bagaimana sistem anti-korupsi di Singapura?", "label": "out_of_domain", "subtype": "adjacent"}

## Out-of-Domain Ambiguous
Generate ambiguous questions that could be interpreted as in-domain but
are actually out-of-domain.

Example:
{"query": "Bagaimana cara meningkatkan integritas diri?", "label": "out_of_domain", "subtype": "ambiguous"}
